ACCESSORY DWELLING UNIT
SUBMITTAL REQUIREMENTS CHECKLIST

PLANNING SERVICES DIVISION

Overview

Planning staff reviews Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs) and Junior Accessory Dwelling Units (JADUs) to determine
compliance with applicable zoning code requirements during the Building Division’s plan check review process. This
checklist provides an overview of the Planning Services Division’s submittal requirements, in addition to the Building
Division’s plan submittal requirements. All accessory dwelling unit requirements can be found in Anaheim Municipal
Code (AMC) Section 18.38.015. Visit Anaheim.net/ADUheim for detailed information on developing ADUs in Anaheim
and for updates on special programs and incentives that support ADU development. For new detached ADUs, your
property may be eligible for the ADU Express Program which provides one day permitting and zero design costs. Visit
Anaheim.net/ADUexpress for more information.

For ADUs and JADUs prior to Building Plan Check Submittal:

☐ 1. SEWER DEFICIENCY VERIFICATION: Provide a copy of written verification from the Public Works Department

verifying that there is sewer capacity to add an ADU to the property. This written verification may be obtained by
submitting an email request to the Public Works Department to PWPermits@anaheim.net. Please allow up to four
days to receive a reply to your email inquiry.

☐ 2. METER SPOT REPORT: Complete this step if you are adding a new electric meter, upgrading an existing meter,

and/or relocating an existing meter. Request a meter sport report inspection online (Anaheim.net/3472) or by
calling 714-765-6847. A utility inspector will contact you within two to three business days and provide you a copy
of the meter spot report. Include the meter spot report on your building plan check submittal.

☐ 3. NEW ADDRESS REQUEST: Complete this step prior to plan submittal to avoid any time delays. Submit a

completed New Address Request Application and site plan to BusinessAssistance@anaheim.net. You will be
contacted via email with a link for payment on the AnaheimNext website. If submitting in-person, please arrive by
11:00 am to ensure same day processing.

For JADUs prior to approval of Building Plan Check Submittal

☐ 1. OWNER OCCUPANCY COVENANT: AMC Section 18.38.015.110 requires the

recordation of an owner occupancy covenant for all JADUs, authorized by the California Government Code
Sections 65852.2 and 65852.22. The covenant for the JADU will be drafted by the City Attorney’s office and must
be recorded with the Orange County Clerk-Recorder’s office prior to the issuance of a building permit. The
covenant will specify the following, at a minimum:
