# HCD ordinance-review letters: Anaheim and Irvine

HCD's written findings on whether each city's ADU rules comply with state law. Our mentor pointed us here ("you'll see every fight between the state and the city"). **Read 2026-11-07.** The PDFs were text-extracted locally, because web fetch only returns their raw bytes.

These are the state agency's own findings, so they're stronger evidence than our reading of the statute. We still report anything a letter suggests about current city practice as **"possible conflict, confirm"**: a city may have changed things since.

## Where to find letters

- **Index:** the HCD Technical Assistance and Enforcement Letters Dashboard, https://www.hcd.ca.gov/hau/enforcement-letters (an embedded app you can filter by city, date and statute; hard to read automatically).
- **Direct PDFs:** ordinance-review letters sit at predictable URLs under
  `https://www.hcd.ca.gov/sites/default/files/docs/policy-and-research/ordinance-review-letters/`,
  named like `<city>-adu-findings-<MMDDYY>.pdf` or `<city>-adu-ta-<MMDDYY>.pdf`. Web-searching `"<city>" ADU site:hcd.ca.gov ordinance-review-letters` finds them.

## Anaheim: March 24, 2025 findings (ADU Ord. 6570 and SB 9 Ord. 6524)

https://www.hcd.ca.gov/sites/default/files/docs/policy-and-research/ordinance-review-letters/anaheim-adu-sb9-findings-032425.pdf

HCD found that both ordinances don't comply with state ADU, JADU and SB 9 law: **10 ADU findings and 7 SB 9 findings.** Anaheim got 30 days to respond. (The letter's own text says "no later than April 23, 2024," which looks like a typo for 2025.)

- **Finding 1, Statutory Numbering:** the ordinance cites **65852.2, 65852.22 and 65852.26**, "deleted by SB 477, effective March 25, 2024," and "the City must amend the Ordinance to refer to the correct code sections."
  → **Anaheim's ADU checklist (Z412, Nov 2025) still cites 65852.2 and 65852.22**, eight months after this letter. That's our headline demo point.
- Other findings cover unit allowances, location limits, sprinklers, subjective language, the local historic inventory, separate sale (the 66341 exception), legal establishment, "granny unit" conversions and approval. The SB 9 findings cover ADU/JADU preemption, unit allowances, objective standards, setbacks and public hearings.
- **Not raised:** the JADU owner-occupancy covenant that Anaheim's checklist requires for every JADU. Our "possible conflict" note on that (Gov 66333(b)) is still only our reading. HCD hasn't backed it.
- **Not checked:** whether Anaheim responded or amended its ordinance after the letter.

## Irvine: January 7, 2025 findings (ADU Ord. 18-05)

https://www.hcd.ca.gov/sites/default/files/docs/policy-and-research/ordinance-review-letters/irvine-adu-findings-010725.pdf

14 findings, with a response due February 7, 2025. The most relevant ones:

- **Finding 1, Statutory Numbering:** same as Anaheim. The ordinance cites the deleted 65852.2, 65852.22 and 65852.26.
- **Finding 5, Zoning Standards:** owner-occupancy requirements and **deed restrictions on all ADUs** are prohibited on lots with an existing or proposed single-family home (Gov 66315). "The City must remove these requirements."
- **Finding 6, Review Timelines:** the ordinance gave **120 days** to decide; **Gov 66317 says 60 days** from a complete application.
- Other findings cover mandatory requirements, definitions, residential zoning, legal establishment, objective standards, setbacks, local building standards, height, parking and separate conveyance.

**Follow-up, March 27, 2025 technical assistance letter:** https://www.hcd.ca.gov/sites/default/files/docs/policy-and-research/ordinance-review-letters/irvine-adu-ta-032725.pdf
HCD notes Irvine didn't amend Ord. 18-05 and plans to rely on state ADU law directly. It also says the city *may* restrict separate sale of ADUs.

→ **Possible conflict, confirm:** Irvine's ADU web page (checked 2026-11-07) still lists a notarized, recorded **covenant** as a submittal item for ADUs. Finding 5 says deed restrictions on all ADUs aren't allowed, but the March 2025 letter allows restricting separate sale, so the covenant may be limited to that. Read the covenant itself before flagging it.
